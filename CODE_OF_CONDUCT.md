# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

MacBridgeWin welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be constructive, assume good faith, focus criticism on ideas and code, respect privacy, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, or publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the community.

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include unnecessary personal information. Maintainers will review reports in confidence and respond in a manner proportionate to the situation.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
